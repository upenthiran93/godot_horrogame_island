# Backstory — Cosmic Horror Bible (v8)

> **Core Genre:** First-Person Cosmic Survival Horror (~2.5 hours).
> **Inspirations:** H.P. Lovecraft (*The Shadow over Innsmouth*, *The Call of Cthulhu*), *SOMA* (consciousness trapped in machines vs. flesh), *Amnesia: The Bunker* (sensory stalker rules), and *BioShock* (an unseen manipulator guiding you over the radio).

---

## The Cosmic Law: *Communion vs. Parasitism*

**The Deep only harmonizes with what opens itself willingly. Taken by force, its gift inverts into an eternal hunger.**

Every person who touches the island's power is judged by this law:
- **Willing Communion (Ego-Surrender):** If you offer your grief, memories, and individuality willingly to the frequency of the Deep, its resonance washes through your cells—knitting shattered bone, erasing disease, and halting decay. The price is gradual transformation: your eyes turn dark and reflective, your voice fades into a harmonic hum, and when your long life ends, your mind joins the **Deep Chorus** dreaming beneath the mountain.
- **Parasitism (Taken by Force):** A human cannot cage, siphon, or harvest a dreaming god while clinging to their selfish human ego. Anyone who tries to steal the regeneration by force triggers a violent feedback loop that twists their body and mind into a starving, immortal hound tethered to the island—a **Shepherd**.

## Part 1: The Sleeper Beneath the Tide & The Ancient Village

- **The Entity (*Y'ha-Nthlei* / "The Sleeper in the Trench"):** The island is the jagged rim of a drowned volcanic caldera sitting directly above an abyssal oceanic trench. Sleeping inside that trench is an ancient, multi-dimensional organism. To human eyes, it is unfathomable—glimpsed only in the deepest flooded caverns as a cathedral-sized luminescence and an acoustic vibration that makes water form geometric patterns.
- **The Black Stone:** Rising out of the sea past the southern point is the **Black Stone**—not a natural rock, but the calcified monolith-crown of the Sleeper's spire. When the planetary and tidal alignment thins the veil between realities, the Black Stone rises above the waves and the island begins to hum.
- **The Three Tide-Stones (Resonance Stones):** Three meteoric black-glass stones that act as dimensional tuning forks. Placed in the sockets of the village's standing-stone ring while the Black Stone is risen, they open a **Resonance Breach** to the Sleeper.
- **The Yearly Tide-Festival & The Pale Shards:**
  - Once a year, when the Black Stone broke the surface, the island's indigenous villagers gathered at the **Festival Ring**.
  - They opened the circle with the Three Tide-Stones and willingly offered their sorrow and ego into the hum.
  - In return, the Sleeper's tide washed through the hollow, curing all sickness and leaving behind small calcified salt-crystals—**Pale Shards**—which the villagers kept in their homes to mend wounds.
  - Over centuries, the villagers grew quiet and long-lived. When they reached extreme old age, they walked down into the flooded **Abyssal Caverns** inside the mountain, sat upon the basalt ledges, and calcified into the **Deep Chorus**—living salt-statues whose minds still sing together in the dark.

## Part 2: The Colonist (1784) — *Captain Alistair Locke & The First Monster*

- **The Wreck of the *HMS Madrigal*:** In the winter of 1784, a British colonial brig—the *HMS Madrigal*, commanded by **Captain Alistair Locke**—struck the jagged reefs of the island's West Cove during a gale.
- **The Healing:** Locke and his surviving crew were dragged ashore dying of compound fractures, gangrene, and scurvy. The mute villagers took them into the **Guest House** and healed them overnight using Pale Shards.
- **The Betrayal:** Locke saw Bosun Hartley's shattered femur knit whole by morning. He demanded to know how the stones worked. With no concept of greed or deception, the villagers showed him the Year Marks, the Healing Wall, and the Three Tide-Stones used in the yearly festival.
- **The Massacre in the Burned Quarter:** Locke wanted this immortality for the British Crown—and for himself—without surrendering his mind to the Deep. On the night of the Festival, Locke and his crew drew muskets and cutlasses hidden beneath their naval greatcoats. They slaughtered the villagers, burned their homes, and pried the **Three Tide-Stones** from the ring.
- **The Iron Cage in the Sea Cave:** Locke dragged the stones down to the **Sea Cave** near his wrecked ship, where he had forged an iron ritual cage to bind and harvest the Entity's ichor by force.
- **The Punishment (Birth of The Shepherd):**
  - You cannot chain the ocean. The breach inverted.
  - Locke's crew were fused directly into the wet limestone walls of the Sea Cave, their calcified skeletons still embedded in the rock.
  - Locke himself—standing at the center holding the Three Stones—was hollowed out and stretched by the Entity's fury into **The Shepherd**: an eight-foot-tall, brine-soaked silhouette whose 1784 naval coat is fused to black, leathery cartilage and deep-sea barnacles.
  - His jaw hangs unhinged, incapable of his own speech, endlessly repeating the stolen last words and voices of everyone who has died on the island (*"If it calls you by name, don't answer"*).
  - TheThree Tide-Stones were scattered in the blast—buried beneath the ashes of the village ruins. One small **Pale Shard** remained clutched in the hand of a villager who died willingly protecting the ring.

## Part 3: The Quiet Centuries

- The island's original culture vanished in a single night. Only roofless stone lanes, charnel piles, and carvings remained in the sunken hollow.
- Mainland fishing villages that once traded with the islanders passed down a single law: **When the Black Stone rises out past the point, no boat touches the sand, and if something outside calls your name, do not answer.**
- Every few decades, when the Black Stone rose, The Shepherd walked the island in the fog, bound to guard the wound he created.

## Part 4: The Billionaire — *Julian Vane & Program TIDEWATER*

- **Julian Vane:** Trillionaire founder of **Vane Bio-Marine**, a defense and biotechnology conglomerate. Brilliant, ruthless, and terrified of death, Vane was diagnosed with an incurable neurodegenerative prion disease that was slowly dissolving his nervous system.
- **Buying the Island:** Through private archives, Vane acquired the recovered 1784 sea-log of the *HMS Madrigal*. Realizing the legend of the healing stones was real, he secretly purchased the island under a fabricated military front—**Joint Maritime Intelligence Directorate, Research Detachment 9 (Program TIDEWATER)**—to keep government inspectors and mainlanders away.
- **The Infrastructure:**
  - Behind the Old Harbor, Vane built **Base Camp** (`A05`) as a supply depot feeding an heavy **Cargo Cable Car** (`A06`).
  - At `300m` elevation on the mountain, he built the **Research Center** (`A20–A21`) to study the acoustic resonance.
  - At `360m` elevation, he built a brutalist **Second Facility: The Neural Siphon Lab** (`A22`).
  - At the **Mountain Summit** (`A23`, `430m`), directly above the volcanic chimney leading down to the Sleeper's sea-trench, he constructed a modern ritual circle.
- **The Cast Hired by Vane:**
  - **Sgt. Elias (Your Brother):** Ex-military combat engineer hired as head of Detachment 9 security. Loyal to his team, grounded, and protective of you (his younger sibling).
  - **Dr. Mara Chen (The Researcher):** Lead marine bio-acoustician. Mara decoded the village carvings and discovered the **Cosmic Law**: that the Sleeper's gift requires willing ego-surrender, and that forcing it creates an abomination.
  - Elias and Mara fell deeply in love during the months on the island, and Elias began to distrust Vane's obsession.

## Part 5: Vane's Plan — *The Neural Siphon*

- **Vane's Problem:** Vane understood the Cosmic Law that destroyed Captain Locke. If Vane stepped into the circle himself with his tyrannical, selfish ego, the Entity would reject him and turn him into a monster. Yet Vane refused to surrender his ego to join the Deep Chorus—he wanted **human immortality on his own terms**.
- **The Machine Solution:** Vane engineered the **Faraday-Resonance Siphon Array**:
  1. Place a **Pure Conduit**—someone with natural harmonic empathy—inside the **Summit Circle** (`A23`) with the Three Tide-Stones recovered from the village dig site.
  2. When the Conduit opens the breach to the Sleeper, intercept the dimensional wave through superconducting cables running down into the **Siphon Pod** in the Second Facility (`A22`), where Vane's dying body is strapped in.
  3. **The Filter:** Strip the pure cellular regeneration and pump it into Vane's body, while dumping the psychic feedback and cosmic ego-death back onto the person in the circle.
- **The Victim:** Vane chose **Dr. Mara Chen** as the Conduit, luring her into the Summit Circle under the guise of a low-power acoustic calibration test.

## Part 6: The Night of the Incident (Three Nights Before the Game)

1. **The Breach Opens:** Vane locked Mara inside the Summit Circle and energized the **Three Tide-Stones**. Offshore, the Black Stone rose slightly; above the mountain peak, the sky tore open into a **Black Halo**—a floating ring of abyssal water and starless void.
2. **Elias Intervenes:** Elias discovered Vane's true schematics in the Siphon Lab. He fought his way up to the Summit as Mara was being consumed by the breach and the Siphon cables began pumping stolen vitality down into Vane's pod.
3. **Pulling the Third Stone:** To save Mara and stop the abomination Vane was creating, Elias lunged into the blinding circle and **ripped Tide-Stone #3 out of its socket** with his bare hands.
4. **The Catastrophe:**
   - **Mara's Fate:** Her physical body was vaporized in the unbalanced dimensional rift, her consciousness scattered into **Resonance Echoes** across the island (whispering through Pale Shards and water to guide the player).
   - **Vane's Fate (The Ghost in the Grid):** Breaking the three-stone triangle mid-siphon caused a massive **Electro-Resonance Backfire**. Vane's consciousness was ripped halfway out of his nervous system and **fused into the island's electrical grid**—its generators, copper lines, floodlights, PA speakers, and radio transceivers.
   - **Vane's Rotting Body:** Vane's physical body remains alive inside the life-support Siphon Pod in the Second Facility (`A22`), half-calcified, with black deep-sea tendrils and copper filaments growing through his veins. **He is slowly mutating into a second Shepherd.** If he does not get Tide-Stone #3 back into the Summit socket before the alignment ends at dawn, his transformation will become permanent.
   - **Elias's Fate:** Touching the raw Tide-Stone shielded Elias from the blast, but infected him with **The Deep Sickness** (black brine in his lungs, coral-like calcification in his veins).
5. **Elias's Escape into the Dark:**
   - Knowing Vane now controlled every camera, powered door, and radio on the mountain, Elias sabotaged **Pylon 3** on the cable car line and fled downward with **Tide-Stone #3**.
   - He descended through the **West Cove Shipwreck** and **Sea Caves**—where Vane's electricity couldn't reach—and sealed himself inside the unpowered **Sub-Level Bunker B (`S14`)** beneath the Research Center, guarding the third stone with his dying breath.

## Part 7: The Letter, The Fake Voicemail, and Your Arrival

- **The Real Letter:** Days before the ritual, Elias smuggled a physical paper letter to you on a supply boat, warning you that Project TIDEWATER was a lie and telling you never to come looking for him.
- **The Voicemail Trap:** After the incident, Vane—trapped in the island's communications array—detected your number in Elias's personnel file. Using AI-spliced audio clips of Elias's voice recorded on base intercoms, Vane sent a desperate voicemail to your phone: *"I'm at the upper station... please come."*
- **Why Vane Needs You:**
  - Vane is a mind in the wires; **he has no hands**.
  - He cannot carry Tide-Stone #3 out of the unpowered caves, and he cannot physically press mechanical keypads or throw manual circuit breakers.
  - He needs a living human—driven by love for their brother—to power the dead sectors, walk into Bunker B, take Tide-Stone #3 from Elias's body, and place it back into the Summit Circle.

## Part 8: Puzzle & Horror Rules of the Three Forces

### 1. The Shepherd (Captain Alistair Locke — The Stalker)
- **What he wants:** Bound to the Sleeper, he hunts anything carrying human greed or electricity on the island, dragging intruders into the dark so the breach can never be forced again.
- **Sensory Rules:**
  - **Sight:** Nearly blind in the dark unless you are within ~3m, directly in front of him, or **your flashlight is on**.
  - **Sound:** Hunts by sound—hears sprinting (~16m), walking (~7m), crouching (~2.5m), and **loud events** (generator pull-cords, wrong breaker bangs, keypad alarm buzzers).
  - **Voice Mimicry:** Repeats distorted lines from dead soldiers (Reyes, Okafor) and spliced echoes of Elias to lure you out of hiding.
  - **Where he won't go:** He will not enter the sacred **Abyssal Caverns (`S13`)** where the Sleeper's eye rests directly beneath the water.

### 2. Julian Vane (The Voice in the Wires)
- **How he speaks:** In Chapters 1–4, he speaks only by splicing recordings of **Sgt. Elias** over intercoms and radios. Once you find Elias's real body in Chapter 5 (`S14`), Vane drops the mask and speaks in his own cold, static-warped voice.
- **What he CAN do (when an area has power):**
  - Turn floodlights and room lights on or off.
  - Lock/unlock magnetic doors and call electric lifts.
  - Broadcast over radios, intercoms, and PA speakers.
  - Trigger loud alarms or vent halon gas (in Chapter 6) to herd you.
- **What he CANNOT do:**
  - Reach into **unpowered areas** (the Old Village before the generator starts, the Shipwreck, the Sea Cave, the Abyssal Caverns).
  - Press physical **keypads**, pull **manual levers**, or carry **fuel/fuses/stones**.
- **The "He Can Lie" Puzzle Rule:**
  - Vane gives you instructions and keypad codes over the radio, *but* whenever a room contains evidence of his crime or a path away from his plan, **he gives a false code** (triggering a loud alarm that summons The Shepherd). The player must check physical logs, carvings, and Elias's handwritten notes to find the true codes.

### 3. Dr. Mara Chen & The Pale Shard
- **The Pale Shard (Acquired in Ch4 Burned Quarter):** A calcified healing crystal from the 1784 massacre.
- **Gameplay Uses:**
  - Glows and hums with Mara's harmonic frequency when pointed toward hidden lore carvings, safe paths, or the Shepherd's proximity in pitch darkness.
  - Can be attuned at ancient stone totems in the caves and summit to emit a **Resonance Flash** that temporarily blinds and repels The Shepherd.

## Part 9: The Three Endings (At the Siphon Lab `S16` & Summit `S17`)

1. **Ending 1 — "The Severed Cord" (Keep Elias's Promise / Destroy the Stone):**
   - You overload Vane's Siphon Pod in `S16` and **shatter Tide-Stone #3** on the Summit altar.
   - The Void Breach collapses. The feedback burns out the mountain's transformers, trapping Vane's half-mutated mind in dead copper forever. Offshore, the Black Stone sinks back beneath the waves. At dawn, the fishing Captain's boat returns for you—alive, though forever touched by the Deep.
2. **Ending 2 — "The New Shepherd" (Obey Vane / Complete the Forced Circuit):**
   - Believing Vane's promise to release Elias and Mara's trapped souls from the breach, you place Tide-Stone #3 into the socket with Vane's Siphon active.
   - Vane's body heals for ten seconds—before the **Cosmic Law** punishes his theft by force, twisting Vane into a **new, horrific Shepherd** as Captain Locke finally crumbles to salt. You are pulled into the breach to join Elias and Mara in the eternal ocean dark.
3. **Ending 3 — "Willing Communion" (Secret Cosmic Ending):**
   - Having discovered all of Mara's logs and Village Carvings, you sever Vane's Siphon cables, step into the Summit Circle holding the **Pale Shard**, and **willingly offer your own ego and memories** to the Sleeper in exchange for your brother's life.
   - You awaken on the Beach (`A01`) at dawn. Beside you on the sand, **Elias is alive and healed**. As the rescue boat approaches for him, you find your voice has become the peaceful hum of the tide—and you walk calmly into the sea to join the Deep Chorus.
